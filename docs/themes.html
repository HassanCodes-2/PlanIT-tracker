New concept

Digital Futurism

A monochrome HUD for your habits. True black, soft graphite surfaces, pure white light — no color anywhere, so the design speaks through structure, spacing and glow.

  • Monospace clock + uppercase date for the terminal feel
  • Segmented progress — 12 white ticks instead of a soft bar
  • Square checkboxes instead of circles — precision over playfulness
  • Thin 1px lines, 8–12px corners, subtle grid texture behind the header
  • White glow accents on active states, streak and checkmarks
  • Two modes: Blackout (black/gray/white) and Paper (white/gray/black)
